Conkies

Ingredients

  • Chopped onions and peppers
  • Ripe tomatoes
  • Eggs
  • Smoked paprike, cumon and chili flakes
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley and feat cheese (for tooping)
  • Crusty bread(for serving)

Steps

  1. Sauté chopped onions and peppers until soft, then add tomatoes, smoked paprika, cumin, and chili flakes, simmering for 20 minutes.
  2. Create wells in the tomato mixture, crack an egg into each, cover, and cook to your desired doneness.
  3. 1. Garnish with fresh herbs and feta cheese, serving warm alongside crusty bread.
