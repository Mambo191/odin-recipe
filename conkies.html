Conkies

Ingredients

  • 2 cups corn flour, ½ cup plain flour, and ¾ lb brown sugar
  • ¾ lb grated pumpkin, ½ lb grated sweet potato, and 1 cup grated coconut
  • 1 cup whole milk, 6 oz melted butter or margarine, and 1 beaten egg
  • 4 oz raisins, 1 tsp grated nutmeg, 1 tsp spice, 1 tsp almond essence, and 1 tsp salt
  • Fresh banana leaves (cut into 8-inch squares)

Steps

  1. Combine dry ingredients, vegetables, fruit, and coconut in a bowl, then stir in wet ingredients to form a thick batter.
  2. Lightly pass banana leaf squares over a flame to soften, spoon batter into the center, and fold closed.
  3. Steam on a rack over boiling water for about an hour until firm, then unwrap and serve.
