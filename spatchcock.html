Conkies

Ingredients

  • 5.5 kg (12–14 lb) whole turkey, thawed, neck and giblets removed
  • 0.45 kg unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp baking powder (optional, for extra-crispy skin)
  • 3 tbsp coarse sea salt (for dry brining)
  • 500 ml (2 cups) dry white wine or low-sodium chicken stock
  • 3 medium yellow onions, thick-sliced
  • 4 large carrots, cut into rough chunks
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced into a paste
  • 3 tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp fresh thyme, stripped and chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh sage, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • Zest of 2 large lemons

Steps

  1. Spatchcock: Place the turkey breast-side down. Cut along both sides of the backbone with kitchen shears to remove it. Flip it breast-side up and press firmly on the breastbone until it flattens; tuck the wing tips underneath.
  2. Dry brine: Pat the turkey dry. Coat with salt and baking powder, then chill uncovered on a wire rack over a baking sheet for 4 to 24 hours.
  3. Prep: Mix softened butter with garlic, herbs, black pepper, and lemon zest. Let the turkey sit at room temperature for 30 minutes while preheating the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  4. Assemble: Spread onions, carrots, and celery in a roasting pan and pour in the wine or stock. Massage half the herb butter under the skin and rub the rest on the outside, then place the turkey on the vegetables.
  5. Roast: Bake at 425°F (220°C) for 30 minutes, then lower to 375°F (190°C) for 1 to 1¼ hours until the breast reaches 155°F–160°F (68°C–71°C) and the thigh reaches 165°F (74°C).
  6. Rest: Tent loosely with foil for 30 to 45 minutes before carving.
